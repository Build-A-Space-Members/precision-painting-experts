## Estimates and process
- Free estimates, no obligation. A crew lead (not a salesperson) walks the property, measures, photographs
  conditions, and takes moisture readings where needed.
- Walkthroughs are usually scheduled within 48 hours. The written, itemized quote usually arrives within one
  business day of the walkthrough.
- The estimate lists surfaces included and excluded, prep steps, primer and topcoat products, number of coats,
  and the schedule. Nothing is added later without written approval.
- Most projects start one to three weeks after approval.
- Process: walkthrough → written estimate → prep days (wash, scrape, sand, mildew treatment, nail pops, rot
  and fascia replacement, re-caulking, spot-priming) → application → punch walk the client signs.
- Typical durations: one interior room usually takes one day. A whole-home interior takes three days to two
  weeks. A full exterior takes three to seven working days plus washing and dry time. Commercial work is
  scheduled around operating hours, including nights and weekends.
- Best exterior season: October through May. Summer work is done earlier in the day while watching dew point
  and radar. Interiors can be done in any season.
- Clients do not need to leave the home. Crews move and protect furniture and work room by room.
- Color matching from a chip, a cabinet door, or a leftover can, with full-size drawdowns.
- No prices are published. Price depends on measured area, surface condition and prep, product line, access
  and height, and the number of colors.
